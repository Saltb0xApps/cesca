A community for modern polymaths

You were never meant
to be just one thing

scroll down

The original Renaissance belonged to the polymath — the painter who was also an engineer, the astronomer who was also a poet. Then the world asked us to specialise.

The Modern Renaissance is the space — and the time — for people who refused. We are a community of multi-passionate makers, scientists, artists and builders who keep more than one fire burning, and who do their best work where disciplines meet.

We gather. We make things together. We share what we know. This is a home for your whole curiosity.

We are a community spanning 40+ cities and countless disciplines, united by a single refusal: to be just one thing.
